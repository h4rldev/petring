Add the ring to your site

Verified inn the ring? Embed the nav widget so visitors can hop member to member. Enter your username to preview it and get your snippet:

Copy this into your page:

<iframe src="/ring/?name=YOURNAME" width="100%" height="32" allowtransparency="true" frameborder="0"></iframe>

Prefer to roll your own? Point any link at /user/YOURNAME/prev, /user/YOURNAME/random or /user/YOURNAME/next, each redirects to that member's site. Full list in the API docs.

What is petAds?

petAds is the nav-link ads-system where petRing members can add their own image that advertises their own website, using a simple widget that anyone can embed onto theirs!

Copy this into your page:

<iframe src="/petads/" width="300" height="300" allowtransparency="true" frameborder="0"></iframe>
